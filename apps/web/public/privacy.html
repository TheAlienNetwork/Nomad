NOMAD

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026. Android package: com.nomad.field

Nomad is a hunting field preview. It is not a legal land-access, season, bag-limit, or permission tool. Verify posted signs and official sources before you hunt.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Nomad Android app used for Play Internal testing and later store releases. The app is built by the Nomad developer listed on the Play Store listing.

Location

If you allow location, Nomad uses precise location to show you on the map and to record hunt tracks. Location stays on the device unless you sign in, then it may sync with your account so your marks return after you sign back in.

Photos

Photos are optional. Nomad only reads a photo if you choose one for a memoir sketch.

Microphone

The microphone is optional. Nomad uses it only while you dictate memoir notes.

Account

If you create an account, we store your email and an encrypted password with our auth provider (Supabase). Field marks, kit, and memoir notes stay private to your user. We do not sell this data.

What we do not collect

No advertising ID, no analytics SDK, no ads, and no sale of personal information.

Sharing

Account and synced field data are processed by Supabase to provide sign-in and cloud save. Map tiles come from public government map services. We do not share data with advertisers.

Retention and your choices

You can deny location, photos, or microphone in Android settings. You can sign out at any time. To delete an account and synced data, contact the developer through the Play Store listing.

Children

Nomad is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account for a child.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Use the Play Store listing contact for Nomad (com.nomad.field) for privacy questions.
